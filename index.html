Methodology

Each row is a practitioner interpretation of how a NIST CSF 2.0 subcategory maps to the ISM Code, IMO MSC.428(98), IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3/Rev.3, and BIMCO guidance. Where the relationship is not direct, the row records a partial fit or a gap rather than forcing a false equivalence.

This is not a legal opinion or a substitute for the authoritative text. Use the row as a cross-reference and verify the relevant clause before relying on it in an SMS, DOC audit, class review, or regulatory response.
